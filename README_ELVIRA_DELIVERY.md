## ✦ What Is In Your Delivery Kit

### 1. Interactive Mathematical Animation Studio (`studio.py` & `run_studio.bat`)
A local desktop application that lets you type any LaTeX formula, choose an infographic template, preview it live, and render production animations in seconds.

* **How to Launch (Local Windows)**:
  1. Double-click [`run_studio.bat`](run_studio.bat).
  2. Your web browser will automatically open to `http://localhost:8055`.
  3. Pick a preset or enter your custom LaTeX formula, select your aspect ratio (9:16 vertical for Reels/Shorts or 16:9 for YouTube), choose your background mode (Obsidian, Alabaster, or Transparent Alpha), and click **Render Mathematical Animation**.
  4. Preview the animation in the embedded player and click **Download File**.

* **How to Launch (Cloud / Google Colab)**:
  If you are away from your main computer or prefer rendering in the cloud, open the included [`elvira_visuals.ipynb`](gdrive_downloads/elvira_visuals.ipynb) notebook in Google Colab (or via the Colab link in your docs).
