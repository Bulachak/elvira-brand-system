01 · FIRST-PRINCIPLES DERIVATION

From Countable Additivity to Score Matching

     

When we write p(x) in a standard machine learning paper, we quietly assume a reference Lebesgue measure dx on ℝⁿ. Yet as soon as we move to continuous-time stochastic differential equations (SDEs) or functional spaces, no translation-invariant Lebesgue measure exists. Every generative trajectory lives in path space, where two measures can easily become mutually singular (ℙ ⊥ ℚ).

Girsanov’s theorem rescues the variational lower bound (ELBO): by matching the drift adjustment via the Stein score ∇_x log p_t(x), we guarantee absolute continuity (ℙ_θ ≪ ℚ_T) across time steps and recover a tractable KL divergence without ever computing an intractable partition constant.
