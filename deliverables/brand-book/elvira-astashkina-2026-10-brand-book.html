The Core Duality: Discrete Tensors × Continuous Manifolds

Why Klimt’s "The Kiss" Is Our Exact Mathematical Metaphor
Left Polarity · Klimt Male Robe

Rigid Discrete Computation

In Klimt’s painting, the male figure’s robe is composed of stark black, white, and gold vertical rectangles. In Elvira’s brand system, these blocks represent discrete linear algebra: weight matrices $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, quantized tokens, and Vision Transformer patch grids $x_p \in \mathbb{R}^{P^2 \cdot C}$.

$$\mathbf{z}_{\ell} = \text{MSA}(\text{LN}(\mathbf{z}_{\ell-1})) + \mathbf{z}_{\ell-1}$$
The Golden Seam Latent Space Isomorphism
Right Polarity · Klimt Female Robe

Fluid Continuous Probability

The female figure’s robe flows with concentric lapis, crimson, and emerald ovals. In Elvira’s visual language, these rings represent continuous probability manifolds: Gaussian iso-density contours $p(\mathbf{x})$, Riemannian metrics, and score-matching diffusion fields $\nabla_{\mathbf{x}} \log p_t(\mathbf{x})$.

$$d\mathbf{x} = \left[\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{x}, t) - g(t)^2 \nabla_{\mathbf{x}} \log p_t(\mathbf{x})\right]dt + g(t)\,d\bar{\mathbf{w}}$$
03 // The Strategic Line in the Sand — What We Strictly Reject
✕ NO NEON AI CLICHÉS Never use glowing blue brains, robot hands, or generic synthwave stock imagery.
✕ NO PILL-SHAPED UI Never use rounded-full buttons or tags. Strictly use 6px, 8px, and 12px architectural radii.
✕ NO HYPE WITHOUT MATH Never publish "10x AI Prompt Hacks." Every post teaches a real mathematical mechanism.
✕ NO LOW-CONTRAST TEXT Never place dark text on dark backgrounds or raw gold text on white backgrounds.

Art Direction System — The 4 Geometric Motifs

Every Motif Encodes a Specific Mathematical Operator
MOTIF 01 · KLIMT MALE ROBE BLOCKS

Discrete Tensors & Patch Grids

Light Mode: Obsidian & Gold rectilinear tiles on Alabaster.
Dark Mode: Alabaster & Gold tiles on Obsidian.
Rule: Use whenever illustrating matrix multiplication $\mathbf{W}\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{b}$, attention weight matrices, or ViT patches $x_p \in \mathbb{R}^{P^2 \cdot C}$.

MOTIF 02 · KLIMT FEMALE ROBE OVALS

Concentric Probability Manifolds

Light Mode: Lapis & Crimson concentric rings on Alabaster.
Dark Mode: Gold, Crimson & Lapis iso-contours on Obsidian.
Rule: Use whenever illustrating continuous probability densities $p(\mathbf{x})$, Gaussian kernels, or Bayesian posteriors.

MOTIF 03 · POINTILLIST MEADOW SPECKS

Latent Cluster Embeddings

Light Mode: Cluster scatter with dashed golden decision boundary.
Dark Mode: Luminous jewel-toned cluster cloud on Obsidian.
Rule: Use for high-dimensional latent spaces, t-SNE / UMAP projections, and $k$-NN classification regions.

MOTIF 04 · GOLDEN LEAF STREAMLINES

Optimization & Gradient Flow

Light Mode: Gold & Lapis Bezier trajectories on Alabaster.
Dark Mode: Sovereign Gold & Crimson gradient vectors on Obsidian.
Rule: Use for loss landscapes $\mathcal{L}(\theta)$, stochastic gradient descent $\theta_{t+1} = \theta_t - \eta \nabla\mathcal{L}$, and ODE/SDE flows.

Logo + First & Last Name Only
04 · HORIZONTAL LOCKUP — DARK
Logo + First & Last Name Only
CLEAR SPACE GEOMETRY Margin = 0.25X

Always preserve at least 25% of the logo mark’s height ($0.25X$) around all four sides free of borders, text, or competing graphics.

✓ DO · HIGH CONTRAST

Use the Dark Lockup (#F5F0E6 text) on dark #1A1815 backgrounds.

✕ DON'T · DARK ON DARK

Never place dark text on a dark background or light text on light.

✕ DON'T · ADD SUBTITLES

Never add taglines or subtitles inside the lockup — only First & Last Name.

✕ DON'T · STRETCH OR PILL

Never distort proportions, use pill frames, or apply neon colors.
